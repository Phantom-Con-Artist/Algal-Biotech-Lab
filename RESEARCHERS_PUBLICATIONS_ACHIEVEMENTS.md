### 1.3 Research Areas & Laboratory Focus
* **Transcriptional Engineering of Microalgae:** Characterization and manipulation of global transcription factors (e.g., PSR1) to regulate secondary metabolite pathways and trigger lipid/carbohydrate accumulation.
* **Abiotic Stress Biology & Programmed Cell Death (PCD):** Unraveling the molecular functions of type I and type II metacaspases and proteome adaptation under salinity and nutritional stresses in *Chlamydomonas reinhardtii*.
* **Carbon Concentrating Mechanisms (CCM):** Investigating sub-cellular and nuclear proteomic alterations under CO₂ starvation to build efficient microbial cell factories.
* **Algal Biofuels & Circular Bioeconomy:** Developing two-step cultivation systems for scalable lipid production, biohydrogen generation via pretreated biomass, and microalgal wastewater bioremediation (phycoremediation).
