## 1. Overview & Purpose

This specification sheet is designed for collecting complete, structured personnel information for the **Team / People** directory of the laboratory website.

Leading life-sciences and microbiology laboratory websites serve three primary functions:
1. **Academic & Global Visibility:** Attracting prospective doctoral students, postdoctoral researchers, and international collaborators.
2. **Institutional & Funding Compliance:** Fulfilling reporting metrics for Indian and international audit bodies (**DST, DBT, SERB/ANRF, CSIR, UGC, ICMR, NIRF, and NAAC Criterion 3**).
3. **Showcasing Mentorship & Placement:** Documenting scholars' awards, landmark papers, and career trajectories (Alumni placements into premier global universities and industry).

Every field in this sheet has been selected to satisfy these standards while remaining clear and simple for lab members to fill out.

## 2. Personnel Hierarchy & Tiers

When displaying the lab team, members will be organized into intuitive academic tiers:

1. **Tier 1: Principal Investigator (PI / Group Leader)**: Faculty heading the research group.
2. **Tier 2: Postdoctoral Fellows & Research Associates (PDF / RA / Project Scientists)**: Independent researchers funded by schemes like SERB N-PDF, DBT-RA, or institute fellowships.
3. **Tier 3: Senior Research Fellows (SRF)**: Advanced doctoral candidates with national fellowships (e.g., CSIR-SRF, ICMR-SRF) or 2+ years of research tenure.
4. **Tier 4: Junior Research Fellows (JRF) & Project Fellows**: Doctoral scholars or project staff supported by competitive national exams (CSIR/UGC NET, DBT-BET, GATE) or grant funds.
5. **Tier 5: Ph.D. Scholars / PMRF Fellows**: Registered university doctoral candidates (including Prime Minister's Research Fellows).
6. **Tier 6: Master’s Thesis Trainees & Project Interns**: M.Sc. dissertation students and visiting summer fellows.
7. **Tier 7: Technical Officers & Laboratory Support Staff**: Culture curators, instrumentation technicians, and scientific assistants.
8. **Tier 8: Laboratory Alumni (Former Members)**: Graduated Ph.D. scholars, past postdocs, and trainees (highlighting their current post and institution).

## 4. Headshot Photography Specifications

To present a cohesive, world-class laboratory image on par with international research institutions, please observe the following guidelines when collecting photos:

* **Framing & Composition:** Chest-up portrait (head and shoulders visible). Centered composition with the subject looking directly at the camera with a warm, confident expression.
* **Aspect Ratio & Resolution:** `1:1` Square or `3:4` / `4:5` Portrait (minimum **`1200 x 1200 pixels`**, JPG, PNG, or WebP).
* **Attire:** Professional smart-casual, academic formal, or clean buttoned laboratory white coat (lab coats look authentic and sharp for life-sciences research).
* **Backdrop Options:**
  * **Option A (Authentic Lab Bokeh):** Softly blurred laboratory setting (e.g., photobioreactor column, green culture racks, glassware).
  * **Option B (Studio Neutral):** Plain, solid light grey, slate, or off-white wall.
  * *Avoid:* Noisy domestic backgrounds, group crop-outs, car selfies, or dark party photos.
* **Lighting:** Diffused, natural front-facing light. Avoid harsh overhead fluorescent glare or direct camera flash cast against the face.
* **File Naming Standard:** Save every photo with the member's lowercase name: `firstname_lastname.jpg` (e.g., `arathi_sreenikethanam.jpg`, `subhisha_raj.jpg`).

## 7. Submission Workflow for Lab Members

1. **Open & Fill:** Open `LAB_PERSONNEL_TEMPLATE.csv` in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice.
2. **Review & Save:** Complete one row per lab member using the definitions in Section 3 and examples in Section 6. Export as `.csv` or `.xlsx`.
3. **Headshots Folder:** Collect corresponding headshot images inside a folder titled `team_photos/` using the specified filename convention (`firstname_lastname.jpg`).
4. **Handoff:** Return the completed spreadsheet and photo folder to the website development team for direct import.
