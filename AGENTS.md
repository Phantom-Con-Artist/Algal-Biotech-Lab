# Agent Engineering & Quality Protocol

> **CRITICAL**: All agents working on this project MUST strictly follow the design, accessibility, and architectural standards defined in [`RULES_FOR_AGENTS.md`](./RULES_FOR_AGENTS.md).

## Core Principles
1. **Mobile First**: Target 320px–480px first; progressive responsive scaling.
2. **WCAG 2.2 AA Contrast**: Strict 4.5:1 text contrast and 3:1 graphical/UI contrast.
3. **Ergonomic Touch Targets**: Minimum 44×44px interactive areas.
4. **Typography & Rhythm**: Modular Major Third scale, fluid clamps, 8pt spacing rhythm.
5. **Fluid Transitions**: GSAP + Lenis smooth scroll and Motion page transitions with full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks.
6. **Data Integrity**: Accurate presentation of Dr. Amit Kumar Bajhaiya and the Central University of Tamil Nadu (CUTN) Algal Biotechnology Laboratory.

Refer to [`RULES_FOR_AGENTS.md`](./RULES_FOR_AGENTS.md) for complete technical directives.
